AgroUSSD

Connecting Farmers Directly to Buyers

The Problem

Farmers like Adigun sell their crops for very low prices because they don't know what the price is in big cities. Middlemen buy cheap from farmers and sell high to buyers, keeping most of the profit. Buyers like Amope pay high prices for produce that isn't even fresh.

The Solution

A text-based (command-line) program that simulates a USSD phone system to connect farmers directly with buyers, eliminating middlemen and ensuring fair pricing.

Key Features

📊

Real-time Market Prices

Farmers can check current market prices for their crops

👨‍🌾

Farmer Crop Listings

Farmers can list their crops with custom pricing

🛒

Buyer Discovery

Buyers can find farmers selling specific crops

📞

Direct Connections

Farmers and buyers connect directly without middlemen

Technical Architecture

System Architecture

Python-based application using Object-Oriented Programming principles with JSON file storage.

The system follows a modular architecture with separate components for models, services, interfaces, and utilities.
